## 🚀 Featured Work

## Dinora — Smart Restaurant Reservation Platform

**Dinora** is a modern, scalable restaurant reservation platform designed to simplify booking management and improve the dining experience for both customers and restaurant owners.

Built with a focus on usability, performance, and real-world business needs, Dinora helps restaurants streamline operations, reduce no-shows, and manage reservations efficiently — all from a single dashboard.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

- **Real-Time Reservation Interface**  
  Clean, intuitive booking experience for customers with time slot selection.

- **Admin Dashboard**  
  Manage reservations, guest details, and daily operations in one place.

- **Smart Booking Management**  
  View upcoming reservations, guest count, special notes, and peak hours.

- **Manual & Online Reservations**  
  Supports walk-ins, phone bookings, and online requests.

- **Guest Data Tracking**  
  Store customer preferences and visit history for better service.

- **Mobile-Responsive Design**  
  Fully optimized across all devices.

---

## 💡 Problem It Solves

Restaurants often struggle with:

- Overbookings or empty tables  
- Manual tracking (paper/Excel)  
- Missed reservations & no-shows  
- Lack of customer insights  

**Dinora replaces outdated systems with a simple, digital solution.**

---

## 🛠️ Tech & Approach

- Designed as a **SaaS-ready platform**  
- Built with **scalable architecture** for multi-restaurant support  
- Focus on **clean UI/UX and performance**  
- Easily integratable with restaurant websites  

---

## 📈 Vision

Dinora is evolving into a complete restaurant operations platform, building on its existing capabilities like automated confirmations, reminders, and analytics insights.

Next steps include:

- **Advanced table optimization tools** for better seating efficiency  
- **Deeper analytics & revenue intelligence** for smarter decision-making  
- **Payment & POS integrations** for seamless operations  
- **End-to-end workflow automation** across reservations and service  

**Goal:** provide restaurants with a unified system to manage bookings, operations, and customer experience in one place.
